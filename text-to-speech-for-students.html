Why this helps more than one more silent read-through

By the time you've proofread your own essay several times, you're not reading what's actually on the page anymore — you're reading what you remember writing. Hearing it read back by a different "voice" than your own internal one breaks that pattern, which is why listening consistently catches errors that another silent pass doesn't.

Works with what you're already writing in

Export your Google Docs essay as a .docx or .pdf, or use a Word document directly. Long dissertations and thesis chapters work fine too — there's no length limit imposed by the tool itself, and you can jump straight to any chapter or section instead of listening start to finish every time.

A few things it catches automatically

Repeated words, double spaces, and unusually long run-on sentences get flagged automatically the moment your file loads — the exact kind of small mechanical slip that's easy to miss when you're focused on the argument itself rather than the sentence-level mechanics.

Free and private

No account, no submission to any plagiarism-style database, nothing stored. Your file is processed entirely in your own browser and discarded the moment you close the tab.
