Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What this site is

Read Aloud is a free browser-based tool that reads uploaded manuscripts aloud with paragraph-by-paragraph highlighting, for proofreading purposes. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Files you upload

Files you upload (TXT, Markdown, DOCX, PDF, or EPUB) are processed entirely inside your own browser. They are never transmitted to, stored on, or seen by any server operated by this site. Closing the browser tab or reloading the page permanently discards the file — there is no server-side copy, backup, or account to sync it to.

Reading an article from a URL

The "read an article from a web link" feature works differently from file uploads, and it's important to be clear about that difference. Browsers block a web page from directly downloading the contents of most other websites (a security restriction called CORS), so when you paste a URL, this site sends that URL to a third-party service, Jina AI's Reader API, which fetches the page on your behalf and returns clean, readable text. That means:

  • The URL you enter is sent to Jina AI, a separate company, so it can retrieve the page.
  • The extracted text is returned to your browser and read/displayed locally from that point on, the same as an uploaded file.
  • This site does not store the URL or the extracted content anywhere.
  • Jina AI's own handling of the URLs and pages it fetches is governed by Jina AI's own privacy policy, not this one.

If you'd rather nothing be sent to any third party, use file upload instead of the URL feature — that path never leaves your browser.

Text-to-speech

Reading the text aloud uses your device's built-in speech synthesis (the Web Speech API). This runs locally in your browser or operating system. No audio or text is sent to this site's servers as part of that process.

Third-party libraries and fonts

This page loads the following third-party resources from their own content delivery networks, which is standard for a browser-based tool like this:

  • Mammoth.js (for reading .docx files) — loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com
  • PDF.js (for reading .pdf files) — loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com
  • JSZip (for reading .epub files) — loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com
  • Google Fonts (Source Serif 4, Inter, JetBrains Mono) — loaded from fonts.googleapis.com

Loading these resources means your browser makes a direct request to Cloudflare's and Google's servers, which may log standard technical information such as your IP address, as any web request does. Your document content is not sent to these services — they are only used to load the code and fonts needed to run the page.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertising served by Google AdSense or a similar third-party ad network. If so:

  • Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info/choices.
  • This site does not control what data ad networks collect through their cookies. Please refer to Google's advertising policy for details on how Google uses this data.

No accounts, no tracking of manuscript content

This site does not require account creation, does not use analytics to track what you upload or what your document contains, and does not build a profile of your reading or writing activity.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
