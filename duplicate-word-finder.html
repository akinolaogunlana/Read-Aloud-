Find the Errors Spellcheck Can't See

"The the." Double spaces. A missing space after a period.These are the errors that survive spellcheck because every individual word is spelled correctly — Read Aloud catches them automatically, before you even press play.

Why spellcheck misses these

A spellchecker verifies that each word exists in a dictionary — it has no opinion about whether "the the" appearing twice in a row is a mistake, because both instances of "the" are spelled perfectly correctly. The same blind spot applies to a double space, a stray space before a comma, or a missing space after a period that accidentally glues two sentences together. These are exactly the errors that make it all the way to a published book, because they're invisible to the tools everyone already relies on.

What gets automatically flagged

  • Repeated consecutive words ("the the," "and and")
  • Double (or triple) spaces
  • A space sitting before a comma, period, or other punctuation
  • A likely missing space after punctuation, where a sentence runs straight into the next
  • Unusually long paragraphs that are hard to follow and worth splitting

How it works

The moment your document loads, every paragraph is scanned locally in your browser using conservative pattern-matching — no AI, no server call, and deliberately tuned to avoid false alarms. Flagged lines get a visible marker directly in the text, plus a running count in the sidebar, and they're automatically included if you export your flagged-lines list.

Turn it off if it's too noisy

A single toggle switch lets you turn automatic flagging off for a given document, if you'd rather rely purely on your own ears while listening.
