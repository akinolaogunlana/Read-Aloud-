About Read Aloud

Read Aloud is a free, browser-based tool that reads uploaded documents and web articles back to you with paragraph-by-paragraph highlighting — built primarily as a proofreading aid, since hearing your own writing catches errors a silent re-read tends to miss.

It's built and maintained by Ogunlana Akinola Okikiola, based in Ose, Ondo State, Nigeria.

What this site is not

Not a company, not a funded startup — a small, independently run tool. It won't have a support team or guaranteed response times, but genuine questions and bug reports are read.
