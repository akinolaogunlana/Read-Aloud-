How this is different from the file-upload features

Browsers deliberately block a web page from reaching into another website's content directly — a security rule called CORS. So reading an arbitrary article by URL needs a small assist: this site sends the link to a third-party extraction service (Jina AI's Reader API), which fetches the page and returns clean article text. That's different from uploading a file, which never leaves your device. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

What works well

Standard articles, blog posts, news pieces, documentation pages — anything built as a normal, mostly-text web page.

What doesn't

Paywalled content, heavily interactive pages, and sites that actively block automated readers may not extract cleanly, or at all. If a link fails, that's usually why.

Who uses this

Anyone catching up on a long read during a commute, editors reviewing a live blog post, students previewing a source before citing it, or writers checking how their own published piece actually reads once it's live.
