Why proof the actual EPUB, not just your draft

A lot can shift between your original manuscript and the final .epub file — a broken paragraph break, a heading that didn't convert cleanly, a chapter that got duplicated during formatting. Proofing the actual file you're about to upload to Google Play Books, Kindle Direct Publishing, Apple Books, or Kobo catches problems your word processor draft never would.

Why EPUB gives the best listening experience here

EPUB is built on real HTML structure — genuine chapter headings, genuine paragraph tags — so Read Aloud can highlight your book with the same precision a properly structured Word document gets, chapter by chapter, sentence by sentence.

The proofing workflow this enables

Upload your EPUB, hit play, and follow along as each paragraph highlights in sync with the narration. Flag anything that sounds wrong — a typo, a repeated word, an awkward sentence — with one click or a keyboard shortcut, then export the full list of flagged lines as a checklist when you're done. Mechanical issues (double spaces, repeated words) get auto-flagged before you even start listening.

Privacy

Your EPUB is unpacked and read entirely in your browser (using JSZip, open-source) — it's never uploaded to any server.
