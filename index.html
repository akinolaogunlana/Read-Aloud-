A free proofreading tool for anything you write

Most typos, repeated words, and awkward sentences are easier to catch by ear than by eye — your eyes fill in what you meant to write, but your ears notice what's actually on the page. Read Aloud turns any document into a listening review session: upload your file, hit play, and follow along as each paragraph is highlighted in sync with the narration.

Who uses it

Self-published authors doing a final pass before uploading to Google Play Books, Kindle Direct Publishing, Apple Books, or Kobo. Editors reviewing a client's draft. Students proofing a thesis or dissertation. Bloggers and content writers checking a post before it goes live. Professionals proofing a report, contract, or proposal. Anyone who wants to hear their own writing instead of just re-reading it silently one more time.

Supported file formats — and web links

Plain text (.txt), Markdown (.md), Microsoft Word (.docx), PDF (.pdf), and EPUB (.epub) — the same format most ebook retailers require for publishing. EPUB and DOCX give the most accurate paragraph-level highlighting, since they carry real document structure; PDF highlighting is page-level, since PDF text has no built-in paragraph markup. You can also paste a link to any standard article or blog post and have it fetched and read aloud directly, without needing to download a file first.

How it works

Everything happens locally, inside your own browser tab. Your file is never uploaded to a server — it's parsed and read using your device's own text-to-speech engine. Flag any line that needs a fix as you listen, then export the full list of flagged lines as a plain text file when you're done.

Privacy

No account, no file storage, no tracking of what you upload. See the Privacy Policy for details on the third-party libraries and fonts this page loads, and how advertising on this site works.

Frequently asked questions

Is Read Aloud free to use?

Yes. Read Aloud is free with no account, no sign-up, and no file limits imposed by the tool itself. It runs entirely in your browser using your device's built-in text-to-speech voices.

What file types can I upload?

Plain text, Markdown, Word (.docx), PDF, and EPUB. DOCX and EPUB give the most accurate paragraph-level highlighting because they contain real document structure; PDF highlighting works at the page level.

Does it upload my file to a server?

No. Every file is parsed and read entirely inside your own browser tab. Nothing is transmitted to or stored on any server.

Can I use this to proofread a book before publishing?

Yes — this is one of its main uses. Many self-published authors use Read Aloud to catch typos, repeated words, and awkward sentences before uploading a manuscript to an ebook store. The flag-and-export feature lets you mark problem lines while listening and download the full list afterward.

Can I read an article from a link instead of uploading a file?

Yes. Paste a URL into the "read an article from a web link" box on the upload screen. This works a little differently from file uploads: since browsers won't let a web page directly download another site's content, the URL you paste is sent to a third-party extraction service to fetch and clean up the article text. See the Privacy Policy for details. File uploads are unaffected by this and never leave your browser.

Which browsers support the read-aloud feature?

Chrome, Edge, and Safari (desktop and mobile) all support it reliably. Firefox has partial support and may offer fewer or lower-quality voices.
